Why I built Declare.

A year ago, my spiritual parents walked through the hardest season I have ever witnessed.

One of their twins, a seven-year-old boy, fell seriously ill. His stomach was swollen, and he was in agony every single day. Nothing anyone did could help him.

In that same season, their finances came under attack. Salaries went unpaid. There were days with no food in the house. Then thieves broke in and stole what was left.

We had nothing to hold on to except the Word in our mouths and the gathering of the brethren. So that is what we held on to. Every day, we opened our mouths and declared what God had already said.

All we had was the Word in our mouths.
It was enough.

Today, one year later, that little boy is fully healed.

What the thief stole, God replaced with brand new things, even the latest models. The finances came back. The house that was empty is full again.

As I looked back on that year, the Holy Spirit spoke to me. Millions of people are facing battles like this one, and many are facing worse. They need these words too.

That is why Declare exists. Every declaration inside it is the same weapon we held in our darkest season, now in your hands for yours.

Your mouth is the weapon. Declare gives you the words.
